# Squeeze Benz

## Game Identity 

Squeeze Benz is a driving game in which the player seeks to cut up the traffic
unscathed as long as possible. The player is not incentivized to drive safely
but rather to move around swiftly. The mantra is: will you risk it for the
biscuit?

## Design Pillars

- The game should incrementally grow in difficulty as the player progresses
  through traffic without reaching a point where it becomes impossible to
  cut through

- One single mistake leads to your end  (just like in real life)

- Car upgrades deepen the risk-reward factor rather than trivialize it

## Game Mechanics

- As the player progresses through traffic, the difficulty increases; the player’s
    minimum speed threshold is increased and traffic is fuller

- Police cars are present and will start their chase if the player goes beyond the speed limit

- If the player crashes the car, they immediately lose and must start again

- A higher speed yields a higher score multiplier

- A high score yields higher monetary compensation after the game

- If the player can escape the police during a chase, they gain a bonus

- Cars are stat-based (speed, acceleration, traction, braking) which affects performance

## Features

- Realistic depiction of car movement to immerse the player

- Challenging traffic jam to invoke the player's sense of wit and reflex

- In-Game store with a plethora of different cars with their own unique stats

- Police chases to further induce the player into a risk-reward state

## Art Style

- Cyberpunk

- Sun bleached asphalt to invoke the feeling of a non ending road

## Sound

- Realistic engine sounds unique to each car, which increases upon acceleration

- NYC Drill Radio (with EQ and reverb as if it came from the car's speaker)
